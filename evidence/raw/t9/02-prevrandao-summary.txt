# prevRandao / mixHash on the Portaldot V3 testnet — read-only check, 1 Nov 2026

RPC: https://test-evm-rpc-v3.portaldot.dev
Methods: `eth_blockNumber`, then `eth_getBlockByNumber(hex, false)` — no transaction, no key.
Raw responses: `01-prevrandao-30-blocks.json` (last 30 blocks) and
`03-prevrandao-history-spotcheck.json` (seven blocks across history).

## Result: the field is present, but its value never changes

Last 30 blocks (282058..282087):

    blocks                     30
    mixHash present            30 of 30
    distinct mixHash values    1
    all-zero values            0
    value (every block)        0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000008e1bc9bf04000

The responses are not a cached duplicate: over the same 30 blocks there are 30 distinct block
numbers, 30 distinct block hashes and 30 distinct timestamps, with 2 distinct state roots, 2 distinct
transaction roots and 2 distinct miners. Only `mixHash` is identical.

Spot check across history, same value every time:

37 distinct blocks sampled in total (30 consecutive + 7 historical); one distinct value.

## What this does and does not say

- It says: on this chain, as served by this RPC, `mixHash` — the field an EVM contract reads as
  `block.prevrandao` — carries **no per-block variation** in the sample. It is a fixed constant,
  not zero, but constant.
- It does **not** say the challenge selector is constant: the selector is
  `keccak256(parent blockhash, prevrandao, dealId) % leafCount`, and the parent blockhash does change
  every block. It says the `prevrandao` term contributes no entropy of its own.
- It does **not** by itself contradict the Discord answer's wording ("safe to use"): the field exists
  and is readable. It does mean we must not claim that `prevrandao` is a source of randomness on this
  chain, and we must not call it secure or unbiasable.
- One RPC's view of 37 blocks is an observation, not a proof about every node or every epoch.
  This is exactly the kind of statement that stays labelled as an observation with its raw output
  attached.
