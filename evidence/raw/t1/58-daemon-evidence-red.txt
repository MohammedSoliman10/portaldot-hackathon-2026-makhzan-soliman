# "passed" only after a read-back shows the challenge closed and the bond unchanged, "failed" only on an
# observed slash, "submitted" right after a send, "pending" after a thrown/reverted answer (retried on
# the next poll while block < deadline, bounded by MAX_ANSWER_ATTEMPTS), and "unanswered" when late or
# exhausted. A per-deal failure must not stop the next deal. The old implementation labels a round
# "passed" immediately and has no retry, so the new cases fail on assertions.
